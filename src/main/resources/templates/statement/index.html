Import a bank statement

Upload your bank account or credit card statement and SpendWitz adds the spending for you. Salary, refunds, transfers to your own accounts and card bill payments are left out, ATM withdrawals go to your cash wallet, and anything you've already added is flagged.

This is a shared demo account, so please don't upload your real statement here. Use the sample statements below, or create your own account.
    Used only to open the files once. It's never stored. With several files, it's tried on each protected one, so upload statements with different passwords separately.
